Beat by Ear: a rhythm game you play by sound, built for VoiceOver. Listen to a bar of cue sounds, then repeat the moves on the beat: a wooden knock means tap, a low beep on the left means swipe left, a high beep on the right means swipe right, and a rising whistle means swipe up. You feel every hit in vibration. Four built-in songs, three difficulties, combos, best scores and timing calibration for Bluetooth headphones.
