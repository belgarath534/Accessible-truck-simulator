Accessible Truck Simulator

A truck driving game you can play entirely by sound. Use Tab and Enter, or the arrow keys, to choose.

This is the first Windows version: the tutorial and the practice loop. Jobs, money, trucks and the map are coming next.

How to Drive

Keyboard

  • N: start or stop the engine.
  • Up arrow or W: accelerate. Hold it: the longer you hold, the faster you go.
  • Down arrow or S: brake.
  • Left and Right arrows, or A and D: steer. Short taps work best.
  • I: read the dashboard: speed, gear, fuel and more.
  • G: GPS: what's on the road ahead.
  • Left bracket and right bracket: turn signals. Backspace cancels the signal.
  • H: horn.
  • Escape: pause menu.
  • F1: hear the keys while driving.

Game controller

  • Right trigger: accelerate. Left trigger: brake. Left stick: steer.
  • Y: engine. A: dashboard. X: GPS. B: horn.
  • Left and right bumpers: turn signals. View button: cancel signal. Menu button: pause.

What you hear

  • The engine gets louder and higher as RPM rises, and you hear a hiss on each gear change.
  • Drifting toward the edge of your lane: soft beeps in that ear, faster and higher the further you drift. Steer away from the beeps.
  • Too far: a rumble strip on that side. Go further and you leave the road and crash.
  • Curves are announced ahead of time with a suggested speed. In a curve you must steer, or the truck drifts to the outside.
  • Your speed is called out every 10 miles or kilometers per hour.
  • In the last 150 meters before the drop-off you hear a beacon, like a parking sensor: faster beeps as you get closer, a steady tone right at the drop-off. Stop there.
  • Headphones are best: the lane beeps tell left from right.

Speech

If NVDA, JAWS or Narrator is running, the game talks through it. Otherwise it uses the Windows voice. You can change this in Settings.
