New sound! Headphones recommended.

• Real truck engine: recorded diesel loops that blend together and rise in pitch with the RPM, a recorded starter when you start the engine, a shutdown sound, and an air brake hiss when you come to a stop.
• New upbeat music: a loud menu theme and three new radio songs (country rock, classic rock, Southern groove). The radio plays quieter than the menu and drops lower whenever the game speaks.
• Radio: three-finger swipe up for on or off, three-finger swipe left or right to change songs.

Please check:
• Does the engine sound like a big truck at idle, pulling away and at highway speed? Any clicks or jumps?
• Does the starter line up with the engine catching?
• Is the radio a good level next to the engine, lane beeps and speech?
