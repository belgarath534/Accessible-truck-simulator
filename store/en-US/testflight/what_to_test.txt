New: hire drivers for your company! Headphones recommended.

• Company on the main menu: hire drivers (1, 2 or 3 stars). Better drivers cost more but deliver faster.
• Each driver needs a truck other than the one you drive. Hiring gives them a free truck automatically, and you can choose which truck in the Company screen.
• Drivers earn money even while the game is closed, for up to 8 hours at a time. When you come back to the main menu you'll hear what they delivered and earned.
• If you switch to a truck a driver is using, the driver swaps to the truck you were driving.
• Also new: police and speeding tickets, and the new air horn.
