New: much easier steering! Headphones recommended.

• Steering assist (Low, Medium and High) now uses simpler handling: steering moves the truck sideways, and letting go stops the drift. Curves still push you to the outside, harder the faster you go, so you steer into them. Medium is the default. Off is the old realistic steering.
• Leaving the road slowly puts you on the gravel shoulder with a warning to steer back. Only leaving the road fast counts as a crash.
• Also in this build: money, the Job Board, delivery pay with bonuses, and Free Drive pay.

Please check:
• Can you stay in your lane now? How many crashes on a delivery?
• Do curves still feel like work? Too easy or too hard? Try Low and High assist too.
• Is the shoulder warning helpful?
