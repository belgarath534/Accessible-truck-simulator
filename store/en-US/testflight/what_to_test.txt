New: music and an in-truck radio, plus drives with an end. Headphones recommended.

• Music in the main menu.
• Radio while driving: it comes on when you start (you can change that in Settings). Three-finger swipe up turns it on or off, three-finger swipe left or right changes the song. The radio gets quieter whenever the game speaks.
• Settings, Music and radio: menu music, radio on start, music volume.
• Practice Drive: one 12 km lap that ends when you stop at the drop-off, with a results screen. Free Drive is the endless loop.
• The pause menu shows your trip: distance driven, distance left, time, speeds and crashes.

Please check:
• Is the music too loud or too quiet next to the engine, lane beeps and speech?
• Do the three-finger swipes work with VoiceOver on?
• Is the drop-off stop easy to find by ear?
