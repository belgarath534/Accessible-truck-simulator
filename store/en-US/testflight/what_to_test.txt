Fairer money: fuel, tows and crashes rebalanced. Headphones recommended.

• Fuel lasts about twice as long: a full tank is around 2 hours of driving, and each trip costs less fuel.
• The Job Board tells you for each job whether you have enough fuel, and has a Fill Up at the Yard button. A drive warns you at the start if your fuel may not last.
• Tows cost $150 (was $400) and take 2 minutes (was 5).
• Crashes damage cargo less: 5 percent of the pay each (10 percent for fragile), never more than 40 percent.
• Also in this build: hire drivers in the Company screen, police and speeding tickets, the new air horn, and the map.
