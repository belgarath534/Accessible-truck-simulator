New: a drop-off beacon, and a better fuel check. Headphones recommended.

• Drop-off beacon: in the last 150 meters you hear beeps like a parking sensor. They get faster as you get closer and turn into a steady tone right at the drop-off. Stop on the steady tone for the perfect-stop bonus. Past the drop-off the beeps drop lower.
• The Job Board's fuel check now uses measured fuel use and counts heavy cargo, which burns more fuel.
• Also: fuel lasts about twice as long as before, tows are cheaper, crashes cost less, and you can hire drivers in the Company screen.

Please check:
• Does the beacon help you stop right at the drop-off?
• Is the fuel check right? Did any job run out of fuel when it said "Fuel OK"?
