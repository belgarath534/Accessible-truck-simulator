New: the Tutorial. Open the game and choose "Start Here: Tutorial" at the top of the main menu. It's a guided first drive on the practice loop that teaches every control one step at a time and waits for you at each step: starting the engine, accelerating, staying in your lane, the dashboard, the GPS, turn signals, curves, the horn and stopping. If you don't do a step, it reminds you. The tutorial is free: no fuel or costs. After you finish it, it moves down the menu next to Practice Drive, so you can take it again.

Also: traffic is now off by default. Turn it on in Settings if you want other vehicles on the map's roads.
