Fixed: tilt steering now works however you hold the phone. Headphones recommended.

• Hold the phone sideways at any angle (upright, tilted back or flat) with the charging port on whichever side you like. Lower the right end to steer right, the left end to steer left. However you hold it when the drive starts counts as straight.
• Easier steering (assist Low, Medium or High): steering moves the truck sideways and letting go stops the drift. Curves push you to the outside, so steer into them.
• Leaving the road slowly puts you on the gravel shoulder with a warning. Only leaving it fast is a crash.
