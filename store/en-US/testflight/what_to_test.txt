New: money and delivery jobs! Headphones recommended.

• Bank balance on the main menu. You start with $500.
• Job Board: three deliveries with cargo, distance, pay, time limit and weight. Pick one, drive to the drop-off and stop there to deliver.
• Pay: bonuses for on time, no crashes and a perfect stop. Being late or crashing costs money, and fragile cargo is damaged more by crashes.
• Heavy cargo (steel, gravel, lumber) speeds up slowly and needs more room to brake.
• Time reminders at 5 minutes and 1 minute. The three-finger tap tells you the distance and time left.
• Free Drive now pays a little per kilometer. Pause and choose End Drive and Get Paid.
• Your money is saved, even after closing the app.

• Do the jobs feel worth doing? Too much or too little money? Are the time limits fair?
• Can you feel the difference with heavy cargo?
