New: achievements and Game Center. There are 22 achievements, from finishing the tutorial and your first delivery to a perfect stop, a heavy load, buying the Road King and hiring a fleet of drivers. When you earn one during a drive, the game says "Achievement unlocked" and its name; earned in a menu (like buying a truck), an alert shows it. The new Achievements screen on the main menu lists every achievement, earned or not, and how to earn it.

If you're signed in to Game Center, achievements are also sent there, along with two leaderboards: money earned and deliveries. Achievements you already qualify for (like deliveries you've made) unlock on your next drive or purchase.
