New in 1.1: eight new trucks, achievements and Game Center.

Trucks: the garage now has 11 trucks, each with its own engine sound and its own feel. Farm Runner ($3,500), City Hauler ($10,000, sharpest steering), Mountain Goat ($14,000, strongest brakes), Highway Star ($24,000), Long Hauler ($28,000, huge tank), Iron Bull ($34,000, for heavy loads), Volt ($40,000, electric: no gears, a motor whine, charges at truck stops) and Titan ($60,000, the best at everything). Use "Hear the engine" in the garage to listen to each one.

Achievements: 23 achievements, spoken when you earn them during a drive, or shown in an alert in the menus. The Achievements screen on the main menu lists them all. If you're signed in to Game Center, they're sent there too, with two leaderboards: money earned and deliveries.
