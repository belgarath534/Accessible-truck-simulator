New: police, speeding tickets and a new air horn. Headphones recommended.

• Police park in every town and along the interstate and country roads. Not every spot has a cop every time. You'll hear a police radio crackle when you pass one.
• Go more than 8 km/h (5 mph) over the limit near a cop and you'll hear the siren and "Pull over". Slow down and stop for your ticket: $100 plus $25 for every km/h over. Don't stop within 25 seconds and the fine is much bigger.
• Tickets come off your pay. The dashboard (two-finger double tap) says the speed limit where you are.
• New air horn (two-finger swipe up) on every truck.

• Is the siren clear? Is the fine fair?
