New: the map! Towns, highways and GPS turn by turn. Headphones recommended.

• Six places: Millbrook (your home yard), Cedar Falls (lumber, up the mountain), Ridgeport (port), Ironton (mines and steel), Bayview (farms), and Central Junction.
• Roads feel different: Interstate 40 at 100 km/h with gentle curves, country roads at 80, mountain roads at 60 with sharp curves, and towns at 50. You hear when you enter and leave a town.
• Jobs go from the town you're in to another town, with cargo that fits where it's from. After a delivery you're in the new town.
• GPS: each turn is announced at 1 km and 300 m. Signal left or right before the junction to turn, or no signal to go straight. Take a wrong turn and it recalculates.
• Free Drive roams the map: before each junction you hear the choices. The three-finger tap says what's ahead and the next turn.
• Practice Drive is still the practice loop.

• Are the GPS turn instructions clear and early enough?
• Is turning with the signal easy?
• Do the roads feel different from each other?
• How long are the jobs now? Too long or too short?
