New: fuel and truck stops! Headphones recommended.

• Fuel stays in your truck between drives, and a full tank lasts about an hour. The dashboard (two-finger double tap) says how far you can go.
• Three truck stops on the loop. Under half a tank you'll hear one coming; the three-finger tap always says the distance to the next one.
• To fill up: signal right before the truck stop and slow below 50 km/h (30 mph). Stop at the pump and listen to it fill. Press the gas to leave early.
• Warnings at a quarter tank and 10 percent. Running out gets you towed to the next truck stop: a tow fee, a quarter tank, and 5 minutes lost.
• Truck stops are cheaper than filling up at the yard in the Garage. Fuel costs come off your pay.
• Also new: truck upgrades (engine, brakes, tires, fuel tank) and the Garage with three trucks.

• Does a tank last a good amount of time?
• Are fuel prices fair compared with what jobs pay?
