New: truck upgrades! Headphones recommended.

• In the Garage, each truck you own has an Upgrades screen with four upgrades, three levels each:
  - Engine: 12 percent more power per level, and the engine sounds bigger and deeper, with more turbo.
  - Brakes: 10 percent stronger per level. Stop shorter, especially with heavy loads.
  - Tires: 8 percent more grip in curves per level.
  - Fuel tank: 25 percent bigger per level.
• Upgrades belong to the truck you bought them for.
• Hear the Engine in the Upgrades screen plays the upgraded sound.
• Also new: the Garage with three trucks (Old Reliable, Workhorse and Road King), each with its own engine sound.
