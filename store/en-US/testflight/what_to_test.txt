New: drives now have an end! Headphones recommended.

• Practice Drive: one 12 km lap. You hear the distance left at 10, 5, 2 and 1 km. At 400 m you're told to slow down. Stop at the drop-off to finish and get your results: stop accuracy, time, average and top speed, crashes and fuel.
• Free Drive: the endless loop, for when you just want to cruise.
• Pause menu (two-finger swipe down) now shows your trip: distance driven, distance left, percent done, time, speeds and crashes.
• Three-finger tap (GPS) now also says the distance to the drop-off.

Please check:
• Is the drop-off stop easy to find by ear? Is the 400 m warning enough time to slow down?
• Does the results screen read well with VoiceOver?
• Anything still hard: lane beeps, curve warnings, tilt or joystick steering.
