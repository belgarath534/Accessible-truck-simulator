New: the Garage! Buy trucks, each with its own engine sound. Headphones recommended.

• Garage on the main menu: three trucks. Hear the Engine plays each one: start, rev up and back down.
  - Old Reliable (yours): steady six-cylinder.
  - Workhorse ($6,000): rough old diesel, lumpy idle, no turbo. A bit less power, but grips better in curves.
  - Road King ($18,000): deep V8 rumble with a loud turbo. Much more power, stronger brakes, bigger tank, faster top speed.
• Buying a truck switches you to it. You can switch back to any truck you own.
• The main menu says which truck you're driving.
