New in 1.1: weather, eight new trucks, achievements and Game Center.

Weather: some drives now have rain or a storm. You hear rain on the cab, the wipers, your tires hissing through water, and thunder in a storm. Wet roads have less grip and take longer to stop, so curve warnings suggest slower speeds. Deliveries pay 10 percent extra in the rain and 20 percent in a storm. Turn it off in Settings if you want clear skies.

Trucks: the garage now has 11 trucks, each with its own engine sound and its own feel. Farm Runner ($3,500), City Hauler ($10,000, sharpest steering), Mountain Goat ($14,000, strongest brakes), Highway Star ($24,000), Long Hauler ($28,000, huge tank), Iron Bull ($34,000, for heavy loads), Volt ($40,000, electric: no gears, a motor whine, charges at truck stops) and Titan ($60,000, the best at everything). Use "Hear the engine" in the garage to listen to each one.

Achievements: 23 achievements, spoken when you earn them during a drive, or shown in an alert in the menus. The Achievements screen on the main menu lists them all. If you're signed in to Game Center, they're sent there too, with two leaderboards: money earned and deliveries.

Accessibility: Settings has a new Accessibility section. Captions show everything the game says as text on the driving screen. Driving buttons add big on-screen buttons for every action (Engine, Faster, Slower, Stop, Steer Left and Right, signals, Dashboard, GPS, Horn, Pause) for Voice Control, Switch Control or anyone who likes buttons; Faster and Slower work like cruise control. The driving screen follows Larger Text, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion, and traffic is labeled, not just colored. The game now also runs on iPad.
