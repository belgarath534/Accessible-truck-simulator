New: traffic! Headphones strongly recommended.

• Roads now have two lanes. On country and mountain roads the left lane is oncoming traffic. On Interstate 40 the left lane goes your way, for passing.
• Oncoming cars, pickups and trucks in 3D sound: they come at you on your left, rush past and fade behind. Drift over the center line with someone coming and they honk: "Oncoming traffic! Get back in your lane!" Hitting one is a crash.
• Slower vehicles ahead in your lane: "Slow pickup ahead, 150 meters, doing 50." Slow down behind them, or pass: signal left and steer into the left lane, then signal right and steer back.
• Lane beeps guide you to the lane you mean to be in.
• Practice Drive has no traffic. Traffic can be turned off in Settings.
• Also: the drop-off beacon, so you can stop right at the drop-off.

Please check:
• Can you tell where each vehicle is by sound?
• Is passing a slow vehicle doable?
• Is there too much traffic, or too little?
