Accessible Truck Simulator is a trucking game you can play completely by sound. It's made for VoiceOver from the ground up by a blind developer, and sighted players can play it too.

You really drive. You start the engine, press the accelerator, brake, steer through the curves and stop on the drop-off. Then you get paid, and build a trucking company.

LEARN IN MINUTES
• A guided tutorial teaches every control, one step at a time, and waits for you at each step
• A practice road full of curves, for learning with no pressure

DRIVE BY EAR
• A real diesel engine that rises and falls with RPM and load, with an air hiss on every gear change
• Soft beeps in the ear you're drifting toward, faster and higher the further you leave the centre of your lane
• Rumble strips you hear and feel on the side you're drifting toward
• Curves announced ahead of time with a suggested speed, and a warning if you're too fast
• A spoken dashboard and GPS on demand: speed, gear, fuel, range, the next curve, the next turn and how far to go
• A beacon in the last 150 meters that leads you to a perfect stop at the drop-off
• Steering help you can turn up or down, from easy to fully realistic

A WORLD TO DRIVE
• Six places to deliver to, joined by an interstate, country roads and a mountain highway
• Turn-by-turn GPS: signal left or right at each junction, and it recalculates if you take a wrong turn
• Towns with lower speed limits, and police cars that ticket you for speeding, only when one is nearby
• Truck stops: signal right, pull in and fill up before you run dry
• Optional traffic: oncoming cars rush past in 3D sound, and slower vehicles to pass

BUILD YOUR COMPANY
• Pick jobs from the Job Board: cargo, distance, pay, time limit and weight are read out for each one
• Bonuses for arriving on time, not crashing and a perfect stop
• Three trucks, from the trusty starter to the Road King, each with its own engine sound
• Upgrades for the engine, brakes, tires and fuel tank
• Hire drivers who deliver for you and earn money, even while the game is closed

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Tilt the phone like a steering wheel, or use a one-finger joystick
• Simple gestures that work with VoiceOver on
• Bluetooth keyboards and PlayStation, Xbox and MFi controllers supported
• A truck radio with its own songs, and vibration that follows the engine

No ads, no account and no data collected. Feedback and ideas are very welcome: the game is updated often based on what players ask for.
