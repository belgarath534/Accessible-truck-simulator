Accessible Truck Sim is a truck-driving game you can play completely by sound. It's made for VoiceOver from the ground up by a blind developer, and sighted players can play it too.

You really drive. No menus pick a route while the truck drives itself. You start the engine, press the accelerator, brake, and steer.

DRIVE BY EAR
• A diesel engine that rises and falls with RPM and load, with an air hiss on every gear change
• Soft beeps in the ear you're drifting toward, faster and higher the further you leave the centre of your lane
• Rumble strips you hear and feel on the side you're drifting toward
• Curves announced ahead of time with a suggested speed, and a warning if you're too fast
• Speed called out as you accelerate, and a spoken dashboard on demand: speed, gear, RPM, fuel, engine temperature and turn signals
• Vibration that follows the engine and kicks hard on the rumble strip

YOUR WAY TO STEER
• Tilt the phone like a steering wheel, or use a one-finger joystick
• Bluetooth keyboards and game controllers supported
• Simple gestures that work with VoiceOver on: three-finger double tap to start the engine, two-finger double tap for the dashboard, two-finger swipes for turn signals and the horn

This is an early version with one truck and a practice road full of curves. Coming next: GPS routes, traffic, deliveries, better trucks, upgrades and your own trucking company.
