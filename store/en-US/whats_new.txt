Duel Mode, your own music and more!

• Duel Mode: two to four players on one phone, no internet needed. Pick a start, a cargo and a destination, then pass the phone. Everyone drives the same route with the same traffic and weather. Fastest time wins, and each crash adds 15 seconds. Thanks to Angela for the idea!
• Your own music on the truck radio: pick songs from Apple Music, or add your own music files from the Files app. Settings, Truck radio.
• Tow truck jobs: buy the Tow Truck in the Garage and answer calls from stranded drivers. Follow the beeps to the car, hook it up and tow it in.
• Weekly Challenges: a new challenge every Monday, the same for every player, with a money prize and a new leaderboard.
• A rival company: Speedy Freight races you for money every week. The Job Board tells you who's ahead.
• Daily streak: your first delivery each day pays a bonus, bigger for every day in a row.
• What's around me: swipe down with three fingers while driving to hear the traffic near you, deer, hills, the next weigh station and the weather.
• Holiday events: pumpkins for Halloween, turkeys and pies for Thanksgiving, presents, Christmas trees and snow for Christmas, and fireworks for New Year. Holiday loads pay extra.
• Career Stats: a new page with your deliveries, money, distance, best streak and more.
• Send Feedback: a new button on the main menu for bugs and ideas.
