The Big Road Update!

• Turbo Mode: flip the switch and go toward 200 mph, with your speed called out as you climb. Earn a bonus for your top speed on top of your normal pay. No police, but it burns fuel fast. Thanks to Reece for the idea!
• A living world: day and night from your phone's clock, crickets at night and birds at dawn. Every town sounds different: seagulls in Ridgeport, the steel mill in Ironton, the farm in Bayview, the sawmill in Cedar Falls.
• Deer on country roads: honk to scare them off, or slow right down.
• Construction zones, toll booths on the interstate and weigh stations on country roads.
• Horn Shop: train, ship, musical and twin trumpet horns.
• Name your trucks and your company.
• Save a long drive and continue it later from the main menu.
• Your hired drivers grow: they ask for raises to move up a star, or quit if you ignore them.
• A welcome setup, upright menus, and a What's New page with the game's whole story.
• 26 achievements and a new Top Speed leaderboard.
• Fixes: vibration returns after leaving the game, a warning before a junction when your signal doesn't match the GPS, and VoiceOver stays on the driving screen.
