A big update!

• Eight new trucks: 11 in all, each with its own recorded engine sound and its own feel. From the little Farm Runner to the electric Volt and the V12 Titan.
• Weather: rain and storms you can hear on the cab, with wipers and thunder. Wet roads are slippery, and deliveries pay extra.
• Achievements and Game Center: 23 achievements and two leaderboards.
• Accessibility: captions, on-screen driving buttons for Voice Control and Switch Control, and support for Larger Text, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.
• Now on iPad.
• Steadier steering: the truck eases back to the middle of your lane when you're not steering, and tilt steering no longer creeps.
