Race your friends online!

• Online Race: race a friend over the internet. One of you creates a race and gets a four-number room code, the other joins with it, and you both drive the same route at the same time. The game tells you who's ahead, and when the other truck is near you can hear it, pass it, or bump into it. Fastest time wins. Needs Game Center.
• Practice races against Robo Rick, at easy, normal or hard, with no internet needed.
• Real voices on the radio: the dispatcher, the Big Rig Radio DJ and the CB truckers now speak with recorded voices.
• Silver Lake: a sixth town up in the mountains, with spring water and maple syrup to haul.
• The Road Train: two trailers, double the cargo and 60 percent more pay. And the Silver Arrow, a sleek highway truck that sips fuel.
• Manual gearbox: shift yourself with three-finger swipes. Songs move to four-finger swipes.
• Rest stops: on long drives you get tired. Stop at a truck stop for a quick nap and a well-rested bonus.
• Advanced Tutorial: a guided drive for the newer features.
