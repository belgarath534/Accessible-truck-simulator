# Accessible Truck Simulator roadmap

Every idea is planned. Decision (November 2026): Parts 1 to 5 below all ship together as one update,
**1.2: The Big Road Update**, built piece by piece and tested on TestFlight along the way. 1.3 stays free
for something new, shaped by player feedback.

## 1.1: Big Update (released)
11 trucks with recorded engines, weather with recorded rain and thunder, 23 achievements and Game Center,
captions, driving buttons, Larger Text / Increase Contrast / Reduce Motion, iPad, steadier steering.

## 1.2, Part 1: Your Company (done)
- Welcome setup on first launch (done)
- Upright menus; driving stays sideways (done)
- Fixes: vibration after leaving the game, junction signal warning, VoiceOver focus on the driving screen (done)
- Save and Continue Later: pause a long drive and pick it up next time
- Driver experience: drivers level up from 1 to 3 stars and earn more
- Raises: good drivers ask for a raise; pay it or they may quit
- Rate-this-game prompt after a great delivery
- What's New page in the game (every version, plus coming soon), shown once after each update

## 1.2, Part 2: Living World (done)
- Town sounds: seagulls and ship horns in Ridgeport, the steel mill in Ironton, farm animals in Bayview, the sawmill in Cedar Falls (done)
- Day and night: crickets and quiet roads at night, birds at dawn, rush hour traffic (done)
- Deer on the road: hear them, honk and brake (done)
- Construction zones: a flagger, beeping machines, one lane (done)
- Toll booths (done)
- Horn shop: train horn, musical horn, ship horn (done)
- Name your truck and your company (done)

## 1.2 extras (done)
- Turbo Mode: no speed limit, a top speed bonus, Speed Demon and a Top Speed leaderboard (idea from a player, Reece)

## 1.2, Part 3: On the Job (done)
- Weigh stations (done)
- Docking by ear: back the trailer into the loading dock with reverse beeps (done)
- Special cargo: livestock (they moo and hate hard braking), refrigerated (spoils if you're slow), oversize (escort car, narrow roads), hazmat (big pay, one crash ends the job) (done)
- Company contracts: several deliveries for one client for a big bonus (done)
- Reputation: unlocks better jobs and clients (done)

## 1.2, Part 4: Voices on the Radio (done; voice recordings pending)
- A dispatcher with a real voice: jobs, jokes and storm warnings, using your company name (done)
- CB radio chatter from other truckers (done)
- Radio DJ between songs, and a weather report (done)
- Story missions ("Get this medicine to Bayview before the bridge closes") (done)
- Radio events from your drivers ("Nadia broke down near Bayview: send a tow or wait") (done)

## 1.2, Part 5: Wild Roads (done)
- Real hills: the engine strains uphill, brakes overheat downhill, runaway truck ramps (done)
- Snow and ice in winter, with chains (done)
- Tire blowouts (done)
- Detours and road closures (done)
- Truck wear and servicing (done)
- Depots in other towns (done)
- Bank loans (done)

## 1.3: planned (November 2026)
Player ideas first; a weekly-update rhythm.
- Weekly Challenges: a new challenge each week with a prize and a weekly leaderboard (done)
- Tow truck jobs: answer calls from stranded drivers, hook up the car, bring it to the mechanic (done)
- New trucks and trailers, including double trailers
- Rest stops (light and optional): a tiredness warning on long drives, a nap at a truck stop for a small bonus
- A bigger map: a sixth town and new roads
- Manual gearbox option
- "What's around me": a gesture that describes what's nearby (done)
- A second tutorial for the 1.2 features
- Your own music on the radio: Apple Music songs or imported files (done)
- Holiday events (Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year) (done)
- A rival company (done)
- Career stats page (done)
- Daily delivery streak (done)
- Send Feedback button (done)
- Recorded radio voices (dispatcher, CB, DJ), when ElevenLabs credits are back
- Later: bus driving (a separate mode; not for now)

## Windows version
Stage 1 done (tutorial and practice drive). Next: jobs, money, trucks and the map; then music, settings and the Microsoft Store.
