# Accessible Truck Sim: Privacy Policy

Accessible Truck Sim does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Your settings (steering method, volume and so on) are saved only on your device.
- Motion sensor data is used only on your device, to steer the truck while you play. It is never saved or sent anywhere.

If this changes in a future version, this page will be updated first.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/belgarath534/Accessible-truck-simulator/issues
