# Accessible Flight Simulator privacy policy

Accessible Flight Simulator collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

The phone's motion sensors are used only while you fly, to turn tilting into controls; nothing is stored or sent.
Your settings and logbook are saved only on your device.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/belgarath534/Accessible-truck-simulator/issues
