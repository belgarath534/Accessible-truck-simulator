Accessible Flight Simulator, first test build.

Try:
- How to Fly first, then Flight School lesson 1 (takeoff).
- Hold the phone sideways like a yoke: tilt back to climb, forward to descend, turn it like a wheel to bank.
- Lessons 2 to 4: turns, landing, and a cross-country flight from Pine Valley to Harbor City.
- Listen for the centerline beeps on the runway, the climb tone, the beacon and the landing tones.

Tell us whether the tilt controls feel right, and whether the sounds tell you enough to fly.
